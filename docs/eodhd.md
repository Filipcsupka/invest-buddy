# EODHD data access

The client lives in `src/data-providers/eodhd/client.ts`. Set `EODHD_API_KEY` in the job environment. It sends requests to `https://eodhd.com/api` with `api_token` and `fmt=json`; it never logs the token. `fetch` and the retry clock are injectable for local tests. HTTP 429 retries honor `Retry-After`; HTTP 402 raises a clear allowance or plan error. `getCallCosts()` gives an in-process estimate by endpoint, counting each attempted request and excluding documented unbilled 402 rejections. It is not the vendor's account ledger.

Set `EODHD_PRIVATE_USE=on` in a private owner environment to use stored EODHD facts in scoring. The default is off; only the exact value `on` enables it. With it off, reconciliation uses SEC revenue and catalyst facts, Alpha Vantage EPS facts, and SEC insider facts. With it on, EODHD revenue, EPS, catalyst and insider facts can also settle those existing rules, and reconciliation applies them to every scored universe symbol, including symbols with no extractor findings; EODHD documents and facts enter the extraction bundle, and EODHD share counts can feed the scanner floor and owner facts. The private data status may count EODHD news dates. The same score rules and thresholds apply. EODHD facts must be dated on or before the score date and first observed by then; the existing insider lead window still applies. SEC and Alpha Vantage remain fallbacks. After changing the switch, rerun reconciliation and scoring to replace stored score snapshots. The switch does not change stored `display_allowed` or `licence_class` values and does not grant public display rights. Keep it off in a public environment until those rights are settled.

Known limits: switching off does not remove EODHD booleans already stored in consensus or carried forward, and the extraction API and public pages share the web process's switch, so private and public databases must be separate or reconciliation and scoring rerun after a switch change. A mixed SEC/EODHD insider fact is tagged EODHD, drops refs and says "EODHD Form 4"; switching off excludes its SEC lines too, and the SEC upsert guard protects only the same date. Share counts use EODHD history instead of merging it with SEC, use `sharesMln * 1e6` when `shares` is absent; SAP and HSBC samples matched that conversion, but other issuers were not checked, and use the current date for availability checks on live pages. SEC revenue fallback silently ignores EODHD query errors; EODHD earnings revisions and news clusters can count as product catalysts without an 8-K event.

## Market view preparation

Migration 102 stores 25 indices, 23 FX pairs and 14 cryptocurrencies in `tracked_assets`. Their codes were present in the EODHD INDX, FOREX and CC symbol lists on 2026-11-06. `asset_bars` keeps their daily bars apart from scoring prices. `eodhd_call_log` records each market bar job's estimated calls by day. The EODHD registry row remains display restricted. New bars take their display flag from `canDisplay("eodhd")` when written, which is currently false. Fresh databases also map SQ to the vendor symbol `XYZ.US`.

Run `npm run markets:bars -- --class index --dry-run` to count tracked assets without requesting vendor data or writing bars. The class may be `index`, `fx`, `crypto` or `all`. A live first run asks for five years by default; later runs start after each symbol's latest stored day. `--backfill N` changes the first-run window. The job upserts bars and counts attempted EODHD calls, including failed requests. `build:jobs` discovers every `src/jobs/eodhd-*.ts` job and excludes test files. The Docker runtime copies `dist/jobs`.

The new market routes use the existing `OWNER_RESEARCH_PASSWORD` Basic auth check from `/owner/research`. They require both that password and `MARKETS_VIEWS=on`; the flag defaults off. The proxy returns 404 while the flag is off and challenges unauthorized requests when it is on. It sets `Cache-Control: private, no-store` and `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` for authorized requests. The routes are absent from the sitemap, excluded by robots rules, and their navigation group appears only after the owner check. `assertViewable()` is available for the page loaders.

Ten live requests on 2026-11-06 checked the three symbol lists, EOD bars for GSPC.INDX, EURUSD.FOREX and BTC-USD.CC, `ETF_Data::Top_10_Holdings` for IWDA.AS, AAPL.US sentiment, a two-row congressional page and 2026 Treasury yield rates. Trimmed captured responses are in `src/data-providers/eodhd/fixtures/markets`. The congressional page worked without a symbol filter; its response has `data`, `meta` and `links`. The yield response has `meta.total` and `data` rows with date, tenor and rate. The coordinator also verified economic events, AAPL.US market-cap history and a two-row screener response. Other fixtures in that folder model those observed or documented shapes; their values are examples.

**UNVERIFIED:** the billed cost of `/sentiments`; live response shapes for bill, long-term and real-yield Treasury rates, macro indicators, dividends, ETF full holdings and ETF exposure filters. Those methods follow the [Treasury rates](https://eodhd.com/financial-apis/us-treasury-ust-interest-rates-api-beta), [macro indicators](https://eodhd.com/financial-apis/macroeconomics-data-and-macro-indicators-api), [dividends](https://eodhd.com/financial-apis/api-splits-dividends) and [sentiment](https://eodhd.com/financial-apis/stock-market-financial-news-api) documentation. Collectors should check a live response before treating those fields as complete.

On 2026-11-06, `/api/user` reported `subscriptionMode=paid`, `subscriptionType=monthly`, `dailyRateLimit=100000`, and `extraLimit=500`. It did not return a plan name, so the response does not establish an ALL-IN-ONE subscription. Single-symbol fundamentals, ETF data, EOD prices, splits, news, bulk EOD, earnings calendar and US Form 4 were accessible in sampled calls. Bulk fundamentals returned HTTP 403. The [EODHD call limits](https://eodhd.com/financial-apis/api-limits) distinguish requests from billed calls and state a standard 1,000-request-per-minute ceiling. No 429 response occurred in this check, so whether it is billed remains unmeasured; the client counts retries conservatively. The [bulk API documentation](https://eodhd.com/financial-apis/bulk-api-eod-splits-dividends) says `filter=extended` adds profile and technical fields without an extra charge. Those technical fields can be zero for older dates, while market cap and beta are not point-in-time.

## Symbol master

`npm run symbols:sync` fetches `/exchanges-list`, then active `/exchange-symbol-list` rows for US, XETRA, LSE, PA, AS, SW, PR, WAR, MI, MC, ST, OL, CO, HE, VI, BR and LS. Each returned exchange updates `symbols` in its own transaction. The key is `Code.EXCH`, such as `CDR.WAR`; `exchange` stores the requested EODHD suffix, since the row's `Exchange` can be a specific US venue. A complete nonempty active response updates names and metadata, preserves `first_seen`, sets `last_seen`, restores active rows, and marks absent rows on that exchange as `delisted`. Missing exchange codes are logged and skipped. The job records a `job_runs` entry through `runLoggedJob`.

The 2026-11-06 `/exchanges-list` response included PA, AS, SW, PR, MC, ST, OL, CO, HE, VI, BR and LS; MI was absent, and the list had no entry naming Italy. The runtime check skips MI. The [exchange API docs](https://eodhd.com/financial-apis/exchanges-api-list-of-tickers-and-trading-hours) state that `delisted=1` returns a separate historical set. This job requests the active set only, so `delisted` means absent from the latest complete active response. It does not import historical delistings predating the first sync.

## Field coverage and limits

- EOD bars provide date, OHLC, adjusted close and volume. Bulk EOD can include previous close, change, moving averages, 250-day range and average volume. The bulk split and dividend responses have different shapes.
- Fundamentals include `General`, `SharesStats`, `outstandingShares`, `Earnings`, `Financials` and, for the two sampled XETRA ETFs, `ETF_Data`. On 2026-11-06, `filter=Financials::Income_Statement::quarterly` returned the date-keyed map directly, without the `Financials` wrapper, and cost 10 calls. `ETF_Data` on EXSA and VUSA had `NetExpenseRatio=null`, numeric `Ongoing_Charge`, `TotalAssets` and `Holdings_Count`.
- `/v1.1/bulk-fundamentals/US?symbols=AAPL` returned HTTP 403 on 2026-11-06 and did not advance `apiRequests`. The client does not use this endpoint.
- The earnings calendar returned SAP's 2026-10-20 report date and EPS values for a historical window at one call. Two one-symbol `/news` requests, for AAPL.US and ASML.AS, each advanced `apiRequests` by five calls. The sampled responses had symbols and sentiment; tags were empty. No extra ticker charge appeared for these requests.
- SEC Form 4 is for US issuers. Its filing pages contain non-derivative and derivative transactions plus pagination. `links.next` is a relative path without the token; callers can use its offset in another `form4` request. Other markets may return 404.

## Price bars

`npm run prices:eodhd -- --mode backfill` requests `/eod/{vendor_symbol}` and `/splits/{vendor_symbol}` for each active universe ticker and SPY. Run it before the daily job so MA200 has at least 200 sessions. Daily mode uses per-symbol EOD and splits requests for exchanges with fewer than 100 tracked tickers. At 100 or more, it uses whole-exchange bulk EOD and `type=splits` requests at 100 calls each, without a `symbols` filter. The job reports its estimated calls, failures and tickers with fewer than 200 history bars. `--only MSFT,SPY` limits the selected universe. Both modes support `--dry-run`, which reads data but writes no bars, actions or job runs. With no key or database, `npm run prices:eodhd -- --dry-run --fixture --mode daily` and `--mode backfill` exercise the recorded MSFT examples without network access.

The active `symbols` table supplies `vendor_symbol`, exchange and currency. An explicit `instrument_ids.vendor_symbols.eodhd` mapping wins. Otherwise the current `instrument_ids.exchange` selects the home listing, including NASDAQ and NYSE hints mapped to EODHD's US exchange. With no hint, US takes priority; a single non-US match is also accepted. Unresolved symbols and missing currencies are recorded as per-symbol failures while other symbols continue. EODHD bars go into the existing `daily_bars` table with `source_id=eodhd`, vendor symbol and currency; changed values retain their previous source and currency in `daily_bar_revisions`. The scoring readers continue to read `daily_bars`. The existing price collectors remain available.

The job stores EODHD's as-traded open, high, low and close. It never uses `adjusted_close`, which [EODHD says includes splits and dividends](https://eodhd.com/help/methodology) and would introduce future information into historical screening. [EODHD says](https://eodhd.com/help/methodology) its reported volume is split adjusted; the job divides older reported volumes by the product of subsequent split ratios to recover as-traded volume. The 2024-06-10 NVDA 10-for-1 split was present in `/splits`, with raw close moving from 1208.88 to 121.79 and pre-split reported volume ten times its as-traded unit. Vendor split ratios are stored in `corporate_actions` as `detected_by=vendor`, with the factor defined as new shares per old share. Raw prices and volumes retain split jumps. A history refresh can revise prior bars; the revision log retains the old values.

Offline tests use synthetic examples and trimmed live responses captured on 2026-11-06. They make no network calls.

## ETF catalog enrichment

EODHD is the only enrichment source; there is no Codex path. Run `npm run etf:enrich -- --limit 20 --dry-run` to inspect a bounded run. Dry run reads the pending catalog and prints the number of unique symbols and estimated calls; it makes no EODHD requests or ingest writes. A live EODHD run requires `INGEST_TOKEN` and `EODHD_API_KEY`. The scanner image copies the module. The CronJob lives in the separate infrastructure repository; it needs `EODHD_API_KEY` and `INGEST_TOKEN` and no longer needs `CODEX_ACCESS_TOKEN`.

The pending API preserves catalog relevance order and joins active `symbols` by ISIN, preferring XETRA. US listings without an ISIN match use `code.US`. The runner fetches each unique symbol once per run and sends batches through the existing ETF ingest schema and shortlist refresh. The fundamentals endpoint cost 10 calls per sampled symbol. A run stops before exceeding 500 calls; set `ETF_EODHD_MAX_CALLS` to another positive multiple of 10 to change that cap. Rows beyond the cap wait for the next run. The default `--limit 20` costs at most 200 calls. Bulk fundamentals returned HTTP 403 on this account, so this path keeps single-symbol requests.

`ETF_Data.Company_Name`, `Index_Name`, `Domicile`, `Holdings_Count`, and `Asset_Allocation` supply fields when present. The [ETF glossary](https://eodhd.com/financial-academy/financial-faq/fundamentals-glossary-etf) defines `NetExpenseRatio` as a fraction (multiplied by 100 for `ter_pct`) and `Ongoing_Charge` as a percent. A lone expense ratio of 1% or more, or a lone ongoing charge below 0.01%, is ambiguous and leaves TER null. When both fields are present, their converted values must agree within 0.01 percentage points. TER also stays null outside 0–10%. EXSA and VUSA had null `NetExpenseRatio`, but `Ongoing_Charge` was 0.1918 and 0.0688 respectively. `World_Regions` weights do not set the catalog region. `TotalAssets` was present for both, but neither response named its currency. The mapper therefore leaves non-US `aum_usd_m` null. Neither response included a UCITS status field; the mapper leaves it null. Missing fields, replication, and distribution remain null. A null UCITS value preserves an existing catalog value. No model fills missing data. Rows with no vendor symbol get `eodhd_unmapped`; rows without issuer, asset class, or TER get `eodhd_incomplete`. An ISIN mismatch or a non-ETF vendor type also marks only that row incomplete. Incomplete and unmapped rows consume one attempt and leave the pending queue after three attempts. `enrichment_source=eodhd` marks ingested rows; `ok` rows enter the existing shortlist ranking, which reads `ter_pct` and `aum_usd_m` from `etf_catalog`.

## Fundamentals and earnings

`npm run fundamentals:eodhd` takes one `/calendar/earnings` call covering 14 days before today through 90 days ahead. It matches the result to active universe symbols using `instrument_ids.vendor_symbols.eodhd` or the `symbols` table. One calendar call covers every matched symbol. Each run stores dated observations in `earnings_calendar_snapshots` with `source_id=eodhd`; the source is part of the calendar uniqueness key, so Alpha Vantage observations can coexist.

The job makes a 10-call fundamentals request when a symbol has never been fetched, when the calendar expects a report more than three days after its latest fetched actual report, or when 100 days have passed since its last fetch. If a fetch finds no matching report, that symbol waits 14 days before another attempt for the same expected date. A new expected date can trigger a fetch sooner. `--max-symbols` defaults to 25, limiting a run to about 251 billed calls (one calendar call plus 25 fundamentals requests). The `eodhd_fundamentals_refresh` table records the last attempt and latest actual report. A symbol failure is recorded in the run summary and the job continues; HTTP 401, 402, and 403 stop further requests. Any symbol failure gives the job a nonzero exit code. The client counter is an estimate, not the vendor ledger. One historical SAP calendar query matched its earnings row; a single-day check cannot establish each exchange's advance calendar window or publication lag after a new report.

Each fetched payload's `General.Sector` and `General.Industry` go to the listing's `symbols` row (migration 101), with `sector_observed_on` set to the first day the current pair was stored. The universe read model uses the industry only for a European instrument whose sector is `Unclassified`, mapping it to an existing sector name in `src/universe/sector.ts`; an unmapped industry stays `Unclassified`, and US names keep their SIC sector. The 2026-11-06 SAP and HSBC responses used `Software - Application` and `Banks - Diversified`; no complete vendor vocabulary was established by these samples.

Quarterly `Financials.Income_Statement.quarterly.*.totalRevenue` and dated `outstandingShares.quarterly` entries go to `fundamentals_pit` with `source_id=eodhd`. `Earnings.History` goes to `eps_history` and updates the `eps_surprise_latest` collected fact used by reconciliation. With private use on, revenue acceleration reads EODHD point-in-time rows before the SEC fallback, and EPS beat reads the same collected-fact rule as Alpha Vantage. Shares use `dateFormatted` for the period end and the later of a matching earnings report date and a valid filing date for availability. Public share-based flags and scanner figures continue to use display-allowed facts. Rows need a safe availability date, and EODHD revenue and EPS reads also require our first observation to be on or before the score date. Historical vendor backfills therefore cannot appear in an earlier replay. The current paid monthly account has no verified public display rights; raw EODHD figures remain internal.

The normal request leaves `filter` unset so the response can contain income, earnings and shares together. `--filter PATH` passes a vendor filter path for testing; `Financials::Income_Statement::quarterly` returned a bare date-keyed map on 2026-11-06, so that filter does not provide a complete ingest payload. In SAP and HSBC responses, `outstandingShares.quarterly` used numeric keys, `date` values such as `2026-Q3`, `dateFormatted` ISO dates, numeric `shares`, and string `sharesMln`. Their `filing_date` sometimes equaled the period end even when `Earnings.History.reportDate` was weeks later. The parser uses the later of a matching earnings report date and a valid filing date, and discards period-end-only availability dates. HSBC's revenue currency was USD while its share quote currency was GBX; a row without a statement currency is skipped. ULVR.LSE showed consecutive half-year income periods, but its sampled `currency_symbol` fields were null and matching report dates were incomplete, so this response cannot safely produce revenue rows. Quarter starts use neighboring report periods when present and a three-month estimate otherwise; fiscal-calendar labels remain inferred.

[EODHD's terms](https://eodhd.com/financial-apis/terms-conditions) permit private, non-commercial analysis under a personal subscription but prohibit displaying or redistributing the data to others, including repackaged data. Its [commercial-use guidance](https://eodhd.com/help/commercial) says apps and websites available to other people need commercial licensing. The `/api/user` response does not prove display or redistribution rights. The owner needs a license decision before exposing vendor data or derived public results.

The trimmed live fixtures in `src/data-providers/eodhd/fixtures/real/` were captured on 2026-11-06 with API tokens removed. Per-call counter deltas were 1 for exchange and EOD requests, 10 for single-symbol fundamentals and Form 4, 5 for news, and 101 for a one-symbol bulk EOD request. The task made about 369 billed calls by those deltas, below the 2,000-call cap. The account counter also changed during the run, so its total increase includes requests outside this task.

```sh
npm run fundamentals:eodhd -- --dry-run --fixture-dir src/data-providers/eodhd/fixtures/offline --only AAPL.US --as-of 2025-10-21
```

With a configured database and key, `--dry-run` fetches and parses due symbols without storing rows. The job itself does not install a CronJob; that belongs in the infrastructure repository. The SEC `companyfacts`/filing-watch revenue and shares paths and Alpha Vantage `EARNINGS`/`EARNINGS_CALENDAR` paths become redundant for covered symbols once EODHD is verified. They remain in place as fallbacks and for comparison.
